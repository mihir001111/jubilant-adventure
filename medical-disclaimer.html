Patient Privacy

Patient Confidentiality

Never share personally identifiable patient information on After Trials unless you are legally permitted and specifically authorized to do so, and only where appropriate under applicable law and professional obligations.

Users must not use the Platform to expose, identify, or improperly disclose patients.

This includes, among other things:

  • Names;
  • Contact information;
  • Medical record numbers;
  • Photographs or videos that can identify a patient;
  • Exact dates or locations that could identify a patient;
  • Unique combinations of information that could reasonably identify a patient; and
  • Other confidential or identifying patient information.

Users are responsible for anonymizing or de-identifying information appropriately before sharing it.

Where there is any doubt, do not share the information.
